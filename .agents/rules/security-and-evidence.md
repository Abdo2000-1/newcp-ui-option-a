# Security and Evidence

- Never place secrets, tokens, PHI, customer records, production logs, private keys, or confidential infrastructure details in source, prompts, logs, screenshots, or evidence.
- Use synthetic or approved sanitized data for tests and demonstrations.
- Do not claim an approval, test, build, integration, authentication flow, screenshot, or review that did not actually occur.
- Link every material claim to a requirement, command result, artifact, reviewer, or explicit limitation.
- Human approval is required for architecture, security, business correctness, merge, deployment, and publication decisions.
